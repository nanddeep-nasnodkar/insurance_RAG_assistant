Filing a Claim: To file a claim, contact your insurance provider within 30 days of the incident,
either through the online portal, mobile app, or by calling the claims hotline. You will need your
policy number, a description of the damage or loss, and any relevant photos or documentation.

Claims Review: Once a claim is filed, an adjuster is assigned within 2-3 business days. The adjuster
will inspect the damage, either in person or virtually via submitted photos, and determine the
covered repair or replacement cost based on your policy terms.

Claims Settlement: After the adjuster's assessment, you will receive a settlement offer. If you
accept, payment is typically issued within 5-10 business days, either directly to you or to your
contractor, depending on the size of the claim. If you disagree with the assessment, you can
request a re-inspection or escalate to a claims supervisor.

Emergency Repairs: If the damage poses an immediate safety risk, you may authorize emergency
repairs up to $2,000 before the adjuster's visit without affecting your claim, provided you keep
all receipts and document the damage with photos beforehand.
